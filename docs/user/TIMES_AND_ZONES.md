# Times and time zones

Every time on screen is an instant the server stored in UTC. You choose how it is shown.

**Account → Preferences → Times shown in**

| Choice | Shows | Example (the same instant) |
|---|---|---|
| My device's time zone (default) | the zone your computer or phone is set to | 08:57 GMT+8 |
| UTC | Coordinated Universal Time | 00:57 UTC |
| Gas-day market time | Central European time, CET in winter and CEST in summer | 01:57 CET |

The preference page shows the current time in all three, so the choice is concrete. It is saved
with your user, so it follows you to the desktop client and to another office.

How times read:

- The zone is always named after the time ("01:57 CET"), because a desk compares times across
  offices and the server's clock is not yours.
- The date appears only when it is not today in the zone shown ("16 Nov 22:57 UTC"); seconds only
  where a screen needs them (audit trails, decision records).
- Hover or focus a time to see the full instant and, when the zone is not UTC, its UTC form.
- A **gas day** is not an instant and is never shifted: gas day 16 Nov is 16 Nov in every zone
  (it runs 06:00 to 06:00 CET).
- Exports and reports state the zone they use.
- Words, months and numbers follow the app's language (English or Chinese), not your browser's.
