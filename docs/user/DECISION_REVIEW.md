# Decision Review

Review is the human checkpoint for every optimization/backtest/scenario
candidate.

1. Open Review from Decision Center or a handoff link.
2. Inspect candidate identity, scenario, economics, constraints, and
   alternatives.
3. Read the evidence pack: status, algorithm, optimality, allocated volume,
   missing inputs, assumptions, and source references.
4. Record your decision: accepted, rejected, or needs attention. The original
   analytical result remains immutable.

Review language is decision-support language. Accepting a candidate does not
place an order, schedule a nomination, or execute a trade.
