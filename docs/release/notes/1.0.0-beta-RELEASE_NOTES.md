# Eurogas Nexus 1.0.0 beta: release notes

For the customer's IT lead and desk leads. Release `v1.0.0-beta.1` (package version
`1.0.0b1`), published 2026-11-20 as a GitHub pre-release on the `beta` channel. It is the
build for customer acceptance, not a production (GA) release; 1.0.0 follows acceptance.
The short list is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md); what you receive and how to
check it is in [DELIVERY_PACKAGE_1.0.0-beta.md](../DELIVERY_PACKAGE_1.0.0-beta.md).

Covers the work since 0.8.0 (commit `2f4d5bc`, 6 November 2026).

## What your desk can now do

### Find your way by task

The top bar has four modes. **Desk**: Today, Prices, Network. **Deals**: Opportunities,
Contracts, Review. **Book**: Position, Limits & P&L, Delivery, Planning. **Research**:
Fundamentals, Spreads, Cargo & netback, Reports. Help & Settings and Admin are in the
account menu. Links saved from 0.8.0 still open the right task.

A page that is empty on a new installation offers **See an example**. The first time a
person opens a page, up to three tips point at its main controls; they can be replayed
from Settings.

### Start the day on Today

Today shows the rows that matter for the person's job: My market, What needs me, My book
and Fundamentals. Traders and operations also see:

- **My gas day**: for each hub, what comes in, what goes out and the net still to clear,
  for the books of their mandate, today or tomorrow.
- **Gas day checklist**: each source's nomination status, mismatches and the LNG terminal
  steps, with the time left turning amber, then red. A status entered here is the desk's
  own record, marked as entered by hand. Nothing is nominated from Eurogas Nexus.

### Read prices the way the desk quotes them

Prices shows the hub board in each hub's own quotation (NBP in p/th, TTF in EUR/MWh), the
forward ladder, and a **Prompt** view for within-day and day-ahead. Each person can choose a
reporting currency and energy unit; it appears beside the native figure, never instead of
it. Times follow the person's own time zone.

### Spot opportunities

Deals → **Opportunities** lists what the evaluators found on your book and in the market:
locational spreads, held capacity, sell-down, event exposure, storage time spread, re-route
through the pool, and a market lane "in the market, not on your book". Each is ranked by net
edge per MWh after transport tariffs, with its value beside it.

### Keep contracts as they are written

Deals → **Contracts** is now the contract list; creating a contract is a separate step.
**New contract** asks for the kind first and then shows only that kind's terms:

- gas supply agreement and upstream tender;
- LNG master agreement (MSPA) and the cargo confirmations under it, listed beneath it;
- LNG term SPA, hub trade, transport, LNG terminal and storage capacity.

Prices are kept as written: fixed, index ± premium, or a formula of weighted indices with a
cap and a floor. Quantities and prices stay in their native units (an NBP contract in therms
per day and pence per therm); the book reads them in MWh. Each contract shows its price for
any delivery month and the index closes behind it. A signed PDF can still be read into a new
contract, every term shown with its page, and nothing is saved until a person checks it.

### Price spreads and routes

Research → **Spreads** has the spread board, a backtest per spread, and **Describe it**:
type a strategy in plain words ("forward spread net of transport tariff between TTF and
NBP") and the product shows how it understood it. Transport between hubs that are not
neighbours is priced over the cheapest chain of crossings, at the tariffs the TSOs publish on
ENTSOG.

Where ENTSOG has no tariff for a crossing, an operator can enter the TSO's published charge
(Admin → Data Sources → Access & certification). A second person reviews it; until then
anything priced over it is marked indicative.

### Understand the fundamentals

Research → **Fundamentals**: storage by country against its history and the EU target, LNG
send-out, pipeline flows and where supply came from, and the north-west Europe balance.
Research → **Cargo & netback** prices a cargo from load to each terminal and hub, with
freight, regas, entry and fuel, in one currency at forward FX.

### See the network and your book on it

Desk → **Network** has an **Our book** view: disruptions and which of our deals they touch,
routes done and to go, and re-route proposals. The map works without any third-party map
service (a bundled basemap).

### Report

Research → **Reports** produces daily, weekly, monthly, situation and question reports as a
single printable file, in English or Chinese, and can schedule them for a role.

### Follow a strategy without trading it

A strategy can be tracked: it runs in shadow on arriving prices and records paper intents
only. A licensed exchange feed, under your entitlement, delivers its prices through the same
tick ingestion the demo's simulated feed uses.

## For IT

- **Platforms.** Windows x64 desktop installer (NSIS); Linux x64 and Linux arm64 desktop
  packages (`.deb`); the server as a container image for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`;
  the web client for your own web server. macOS is not built.
- **Versions you will see.** About box, sign-in screen and `/api/health`: `1.0.0-beta`.
  File names and `/api/runtime/release`: `1.0.0-beta.1`. Windows Installed apps:
  `1.0.0-beta.1`. Debian package version: `1.0.0~beta1`, so the later 1.0.0 installs as an
  upgrade.
- **Database.** Migrations run to head `0076_gas_day_nominations`. Back up before upgrading
  from 0.8.0.
- **Faster pages.** Reads answer `304 Not Modified` when nothing changed (ETag), static map
  data is compressed and revalidated, the Network page stays loaded while people work
  elsewhere, and coming back to a page shows the last good data at once.
- **Public API surface:** 277 paths / 310 operations (measured from the application at this
  commit). The dataset builder and the agents workspace are no longer served unless
  `EUROGAS_NEXUS_DEFERRED_CAPABILITIES` turns them on.
- **Data sources.** 42 sources are registered; public ones (ENTSOG, GIE) start only after
  an administrator approves them for the firm. Licensed market data (Trayport, ICE Endex,
  EEX, Argus, ICIS, Kpler, Platts) arrives only through your own entitlement and credentials.

## Not in this release, or limited

- Weather-driven demand is not on screen.
- Imbalance (cash-out) prices are not ingested.
- The LNG sea-distance table is empty; cargo studies need the firm's freight routes entered.
- No order entry, no nomination, no settlement. This is decision support by design.
- Region scope (a person's market region) is not enforced. Book visibility by region is.
- Installers are **unsigned**. Your firm may sign them with its own certificate; the release
  records the signing state of every file.
- Prices on the demo server are simulated and say so; they are never market prices.
- Gates still open, to be passed with you: **G15** independent penetration test of your
  deployment, **G16** certification of each licensed source under your entitlement, **G18**
  acceptance by your traders, analysts and risk staff.
