# 0.7.0 engineering log

Engineering notes for 0.7.0, for reviewers and auditors. The user-facing summary is in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Schema

- `0055_desk_operations`: contract lifecycle columns (`retired_at_utc`, `retired_by`,
  `retire_reason`), mandate hedge band and instruments, LNG terminal tariff provenance
  (regime, capacity basis, per-cargo and per-class charges, source URL, SHA-256,
  effective date), and the tables `tso_tariff_publications`, `support_reports`,
  `notification_channels`, `notification_outbox`, `notification_deliveries`. It also
  widens ten PostgreSQL columns that had drifted narrower than their models
  (`ingestion_runs.status` 16 → 32 rejected `SUCCEEDED_WITH_WARNINGS`; identity, session
  and OIDC columns 64 → 80). SQLite ignores VARCHAR lengths, which is why no test saw it;
  `tests/integration/test_schema_matches_models_postgres.py` now fails on any model column
  wider than the migrated schema, a missing table or a missing column.
- `0056_hedge_approvals`: one row per contract with the risk-approved band.

## API

Census 231 paths / 248 operations (from 221 / 237). Each new path is listed with its
authority in `docs/architecture/API_CONTRACT_EVOLUTION_POLICY.md`. Writes that set limits
(credit lines, mandates, hedge approvals) require the REVIEWER role itself, not a higher
rank, because traders are analysts and outrank reviewers.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Retire, never delete.** A contract's revisions and audit are evidence; retiring
  removes it from positions, risk, analysis context and the map. The edit token covers
  the lifecycle columns so a retire races cleanly with an edit.
- **Hedge bands live in two places on purpose.** The mandate's band governs a book; a
  deal approval governs one contract. The trader books the contract; risk owns the
  approval (segregation of duties). Cover is measured from trades the ETRM links to the
  contract (`linked_resource_id`), so nothing is inferred from hub and month alone.
- **Daily balance** spreads every trade over its delivery days (a month sold is
  1/30 of its volume each day), scales contract supply by the measured
  allocated/nominated ratio over 30 gas days, and prices a residual only at the desk's
  own cash-out spreads; it does not invent system prices.
- **Sell-down** is a newsvendor problem: Q* = μ + σΦ⁻¹(c_u/(c_u + c_o)), rounded to lots,
  with σ measured from ≥ 10 gas days of allocations or, failing that, the nomination
  tolerance read as a 90 % band (and the response says so).
- **LNG terminals price capacity differently**, so each terminal product carries its
  regime: Fluxys Zeebrugge regulated and indexed monthly; Elengy ATTM7 regulated by cargo
  class for term and SMART SPOT (in force to the 1 April 2028 update, CRE deliberation
  2025-41); DET auctioned; Gate, Dunkerque and Grain negotiated (the desk's own terms). A
  cargo class the terminal does not list is refused, not interpolated. Held long-term
  capacity is sunk: only its variable cost enters a bid.
- **Notifications are email only** (the customer's choice): an outbox with a dedupe key,
  three attempts five minutes apart, a delivery record per attempt.
- **Outbound HTTP** keeps `trust_env=False` (no stray `.netrc` reaches an official
  download) but passes exactly `HTTPS_PROXY`/`NO_PROXY` and `SSL_CERT_FILE`, so the
  connectors work on a proxied corporate network.

## Found by walking each role through the client

Signed in as a trader, a risk officer, an IT desk member and an administrator and doing
their work by typing and clicking (scripted with Playwright, screenshots in the manual):

- New drafts carried invented facts that could be saved; now blank and blocked.
- Negative hedge ratios (-222 %) for trades adding to a position; now "adds 222 %".
- Cross-mandate false breaches for a trader with two books.
- Filled trades showed their unfilled remainder (0 MWh).
- Principal ids instead of names in three places.
- The IT inbox three clicks deep; now its own tab.
- Chromium's anonymous `::details-content` box could not shrink, so for roles with six
  workspaces the account menu left a 1280–1680 px screen; `display: contents` on it.
- Unstyled buttons fell back to the browser bevel; a base button style now sits in the
  lowest cascade layer so every component's own style still wins.
- `--eg-border` was never defined; cards fell back to a dark-theme colour.

## Removed

- `src/eurogas_nexus/core/errors.py` and `src/eurogas_nexus/domain/agents/model_routing.py`
  (imported by nothing).
- Webhook and SMS notification channels (not wanted; their encrypted-secret columns went
  with them before any release carried them).

## Performance

At trading-house scale (300 contracts, 5,000 trades) the ladder, credit and mandate reads
take 187–228 ms; mark-to-market reuses the ladder's month split (207 → 187 ms). See
`docs/operations/PERFORMANCE_BASELINE.md`.
