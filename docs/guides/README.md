Common to everyone:

- **Account menu** (your initials, top right): language, theme, keyboard shortcuts,
  Settings, sign out. Press `?` for shortcuts and `Ctrl+K` (`⌘K` on macOS) for the command
  palette.
- **Settings → My preferences:** language, theme, default market view, quote layout and
  profit colour (green or red for profit). They are saved to your account and follow you
  to the desktop client and other offices.
- Every figure carries its source and age; a simulated or stale number is labelled as such.
